1629X Worlds Robot

Mechanical design and CAD for our 2025 VEX Worlds robot.

I did the mechanical design for team 1629X's 2025 season. The whole robot was modeled in CAD before we cut anything, so we could check clearances, swap subsystems in and out, and hand parts off to be machined.

1629X Codebase

Autonomous control for the 2025 robot.

The second-generation autonomous stack for 1629X. It used Monte Carlo localization to track the robot's position on the field and a RAMSETE controller to follow paths during the autonomous period.
